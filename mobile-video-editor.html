🤖 Android (Chrome) — How to Install

  1. Tap the ⋮ (three dots) menu at the top right of Chrome
  2. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
  3. Tap Install — done! The icon will appear on your Home Screen 🎉

🍎 iPhone (Safari) — How to Install

  1. Tap the Share ⬆️ button at the bottom
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap Add at the top — the icon will appear on your Home Screen 🎉

Note: If you are viewing this inside the Muse app, first open this page in your phone's Chrome or Safari browser — then these steps will work.
